Most scouting reports tell you the opponent's weakness. Mine used to stop there — useless if you don't know whether your own team can exploit it.

I built an esports scouting agent on Hindsight persistent memory. It tracks the opponent AND our own team, same schema, two memory banks.

Before: "They're weak at long range." So what?
After: "Our long-range beats their close-range — but their early aggression hits our own weak spot."

That second line only exists because the agent reasoned across two banks at once, not one in isolation.
